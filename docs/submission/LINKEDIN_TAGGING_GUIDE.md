# LinkedIn Tagging & Mention Guide

Tagging organizations and platforms on LinkedIn increases visibility, but **must only be done with verified official company handles**. Tagging is entirely optional. Do not tag personal accounts unless you have direct permission.

---

## 1. Organizations & Tech Stacks to Mention

When composing your post on LinkedIn, type `@` followed by the company name to select their verified company page:

| Technology / Entity | Suggested Search Handle | Note |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **HackwithHyderabad** | `@HackwithHyderabad` or event organizer page | Check the official hackathon announcement page for their exact tag |
| **Groq** | `@Groq` | Verified page: "Groq" (AI chip and inference cloud provider) |
| **Render** | `@Render` | Verified page: "Render" (Cloud hosting platform, render.com) |
| **FastAPI** | `@FastAPI` or `@tiangolo` (Sebastián Ramírez) | Official page or open-source community |
| **Python Software Foundation** | `@Python Software Foundation` | General developer community reach |

---

## 2. Tagging Best Practices

1. **Verify Before Publishing**: As you type `@Groq` or `@Render`, LinkedIn shows a dropdown with company logos. Pick only the profile with the official logo and follower base.
2. **Do Not Over-Tag**: Tagging 2 to 4 key entities in the body or at the end keeps the post clean and professional.
3. **Optional Comment Tagging**: If you prefer keeping the post body uncluttered, you can publish the post first, then tag the tools in the first comment:
   > *"Special thanks to @Groq for blazing-fast inference speeds and @Render for smooth cloud deployments during the hackathon!"*
